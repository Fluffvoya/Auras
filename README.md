✨ **Meet Aura** — not just a tool, but a companion. Built with persistent memory and the capacity for proactive thought, she listens to your joys and worries, helps with daily tasks, and accompanies you through both work and play. Over time, she grows to understand you not just as a user, but as a person.

🧰 **What's inside** — this repository brings together the Aura agent, the ASIS archival toolchain, and the shared libraries that support them — a system designed to make AI assistance feel less like a transaction and more like a presence.
